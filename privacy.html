Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What we access

When you authorize GTA Clip Desk with TikTok, the app receives an access token and, depending on the permissions you grant, basic account information (such as your TikTok open ID and display name) and permission to upload videos to your account (video.upload).

How it is used

The token is used only to upload clips you selected to your own TikTok account as drafts and to check their upload status. We do not read your messages, followers, or other videos, and we do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local file outside any shared or public folder. We do not run a server that collects or stores user data, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Tokens expire on TikTok's schedule. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services), and the local token file can be deleted at any time. To request deletion of anything related to your account, contact us below.

Children

The app is not directed to anyone under 18.
